- Hammam Qassim, [*Classical Simulations of Quantum Systems Using Stabilizer Decompositions*](https://dspacemainprd01.lib.uwaterloo.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/aff1f984-003a-4801-bd9c-608f0bfb8743/content), PhD thesis, University of Waterloo, 2020. The conjecture $\chi(\mathrm{cat}_6^{\otimes2})\leq8$ is equation (4.30), near the end of §4.3; the conjectural exponent $3/8$ is equation (4.31).

- Hammam Qassim, Hakop Pashayan, and David Gosset, [*Improved Upper Bounds on the Stabilizer Rank of Magic States*](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2021-12-20-606/), *Quantum* **5**, 606 (2021). The three-term $\mathrm{cat}_6$ construction appears in the main text, and the appendix proves the exact value $\chi(\mathrm{cat}_6)=3$.

- Sergey Bravyi, Graeme Smith, and John A. Smolin, [*Trading Classical and Quantum Computational Resources*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.6.021043), *Physical Review X* **6**, 021043 (2016). Background on exact stabilizer rank and magic-state simulation.

- Sergey Bravyi, David Browne, Padraic Calpin, Earl Campbell, David Gosset, and Mark Howard, [*Simulation of Quantum Circuits by Low-Rank Stabilizer Decompositions*](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2019-09-02-181/), *Quantum* **3**, 181 (2019). Background on stabilizer decompositions, stabilizer fidelity, and stabilizer extent.

- Benjamin Lovitz and Vincent Steffan, [*New Techniques for Bounding Stabilizer Rank*](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2022-04-20-692), *Quantum* **6**, 692 (2022). General lower-bound context; it does not determine the small instance considered here.
